# Archive

This folder contains the **original step-by-step lab guides** from the initial version of the bootcamp.

These files have been preserved for reference but are **no longer the active participant materials**.

## What happened

The lab was restructured from a 6-step self-guided format into a 4-activity format optimised for a 2-hour instructor-led session:

| Old (steps/) | New (lab/) |
|---|---|
| Step 1 – Use Case Creation | **Activity 1** – Use Case Creation |
| Step 2 – Risk & Compliance Review | **Activity 2** – Risk & Compliance Review |
| Steps 3, 4, 5 – AI Lifecycle | **Activity 3** – AI Lifecycle *(instructor-led walkthrough)* |
| Step 6 – Incident Management | **Activity 4** – Incident Management *(participant hands-on)* |

## Where to find active content

All current participant-facing guides live in **[`../lab/`](../lab/)**.

Instructor setup instructions are in **[`../instructor-prep.md`](../instructor-prep.md)**.

## Why these files are kept

- Cross-reference for instructors who want full technical detail
- Appendix guides in `lab/3-ai-lifecycle/appendix/` and `lab/4-incident-management/appendix/` were copied directly from these originals
- Useful if the lab is ever expanded back to a self-paced format
